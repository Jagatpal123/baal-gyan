Privacy Policy for Baal Gyan Pro Ultra

Last updated: October 03, 2026

Baal Gyan Pro Ultra ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application Baal Gyan Pro Ultra (Package Name: com.jagatpal.balgyanultra). This policy explains our practices regarding data collection and privacy when using our educational app.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, address, or email) to access the app.

  • Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly in real-time for speech translation and phonetics within the app. Audio data is not recorded, stored, or sent to external servers.
  • Device Diagnostics: Basic non-identifiable technical logs may be processed by standard SDKs to ensure stability.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays contextual ads using Google AdMob. Advertisements are served in full compliance with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA standards, ensuring safe and appropriate content for children and general audiences.

For more details, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Baal Gyan Pro Ultra is built as a child-safe educational platform. We do not collect personal data from children under 13. All educational exercises function directly on the device.

4. Permissions Summary

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: Real-time speech input for educational pronunciation.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Loading ads and standard app resources.
